There is a particular kind of afternoon where the editor stops feeling like a
tool and starts feeling like an instrument. You stop *thinking about* the code
and start **playing** it. That is the flow state, and vibe coding is mostly the
practice of getting back there on purpose.

## Stop negotiating with yourself

Most lost time isn't spent coding—it's spent deciding whether to code. The vibe
coder removes the negotiation entirely. Music on, notifications off, one tab,
one intention. The decision is made before you sit down.

> The goal is not to work harder. It is to lower the activation energy until
> starting is the easy part.

## Let the loop carry you

A good session has a rhythm: a small change, a quick run, a tiny win, repeat.
Keep the loop short enough that feedback feels instant. When the loop is tight,
momentum compounds—and momentum is the whole point.

1. Pick the smallest thing that would feel good to finish.
2. Ship it in under ten minutes.
3. Let the win pull you into the next one.

## The soundtrack is not optional

Ambient beats give your brain a steady tempo to lean on. It is scaffolding for
attention. Press play, and let the first thirty seconds be the only discipline
you need.
