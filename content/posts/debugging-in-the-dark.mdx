Every coder knows the bug that refuses to die. You have read the same five lines
forty times. The vibe-coding answer is not to try harder—it is to change the
weather in the room.

## Dim the lights, widen the mind

Harsh light narrows focus, which is great for execution and terrible for
insight. Stuck problems need peripheral vision. Dim the room, put on something
without lyrics, and let your gaze go soft.

## Ambient music as a thinking partner

Eno's airport music was designed to be *as ignorable as it is interesting.* That
is precisely the texture you want while debugging: a calm backdrop that keeps
the panic at bay without demanding attention.

1. State the bug out loud, as if to a friend.
2. Write down what you *know* is true.
3. Distrust exactly one of those things.

> Most stubborn bugs live inside an assumption you forgot you were making.

## Walk away to win

If twenty minutes pass with no progress, leave. The fix arrives on the walk, in
the shower, somewhere your hands are busy and your mind is loose. Coming back is
not giving up—it is the move.
