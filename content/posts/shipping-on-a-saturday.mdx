There is no purer form of vibe coding than the Saturday build. No standup, no
ticket, no roadmap—just you, a half-formed idea, and a record you love. This is
where the joy lives.

## Lower the stakes, raise the fun

The weekend project answers to no one. That freedom is the entire feature. Build
the silly thing. Build the thing only you would use. The lack of consequence is
what lets you be brave.

> The projects with no point are often the ones that teach you the most.

## Pick music with momentum

Saturday is for records that move. Something a little psychedelic, a little
hooky—music that makes the hours disappear and the cursor keep moving.

- One idea.
- One afternoon.
- One thing that works by sundown.

## Ship before you sleep

Deploy it, even if only you will ever see it. The act of shipping turns a doodle
into a thing that exists. And a thing that exists can grow—next Saturday, or
never. Both are fine.
