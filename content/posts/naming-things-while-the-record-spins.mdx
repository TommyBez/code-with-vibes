They say the two hardest problems in computer science are cache invalidation,
naming things, and off-by-one errors. Vibe coding does not solve them—but it
does make sitting with them more pleasant.

## Naming is listening

A good name is one you can hear. Say it out loud. Does it sound like the thing it
does? Modal jazz is perfect for this: it is unhurried, it leaves space, and space
is exactly what you need to let the right word surface.

> Don't force the name. Describe the behavior plainly, then delete every word
> that isn't doing work.

## The record is your timer

An album has a natural arc—a beginning, a middle, and an end. Let it be your
session boundary. When *Kind of Blue* finishes, the feature is either done or
it is tomorrow's problem. Either way, you stop.

## Leave it humming

The best place to stop is mid-melody, with the next move obvious. Tomorrow you
will sit down, press play, and pick the thread right back up.
