The fastest prototypes are not the most planned ones. They are the ones built
while the idea is still warm. Vibe coding is the art of moving at the speed of
feeling—getting something on the screen before the doubt catches up.

## Rough is a feature

A prototype that looks finished invites finished-level critique. Keep it scrappy
on purpose. Hard-code the data. Fake the API. Use the ugliest possible color.
The point is to **feel** the interaction, not to defend it.

## Taste is a fast compiler

You already know when something feels right. That instinct is faster than any
test suite for the questions that matter early on: *Is this fun? Is it obvious?
Does it spark anything?* Trust it first, verify it later.

- Build the happy path only.
- Click it like a stranger would.
- If it bores you, it will bore everyone.

> Synths and four-on-the-floor make great prototyping music. The tempo keeps
> your hands moving before your inner critic can sit down.

## Then, and only then, get serious

Once the vibe is confirmed, you can pour in the rigor: types, tests, edge cases.
But by then you are hardening something you already believe in—and that is a
much better use of discipline.
